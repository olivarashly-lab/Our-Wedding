Capítulos de nuestro amor

Our Love Story

A story written between the Philippines and Poland. Select each chapter to turn the page.

We met on a dating app. At the time, Ashly was working in consulting, and Elkhan invited her for coffee near her workplace. One conversation became another, and soon there was something neither of us expected.

After a week, Elkhan asked Ashly to be his girlfriend. Then he returned to Poland. Ashly wondered whether a love that had just begun could really survive the distance. Three months later, Elkhan came back to see her — and proved that he meant it.

We traveled together to El Nido, Philippines. Amid turquoise waters and quiet days, our relationship grew into a shared dream: we wanted to build a life together, not just collect beautiful moments.

Ashly applied for a student visa and received a scholarship to study Management in Poland. Meanwhile, Elkhan was working as an Assistant Vice President (AVP) in banking in Warsaw. Between his demanding career and Ashly’s studies, he always made time for us, and together we began to make a home in a new country.

Eight months later, Ashly began working at a technology company. With work, study, ordinary routines, and the joy of being together, we knew we were ready for our next chapter: marriage.
